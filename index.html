Can you explain how to find the nature of roots of a quadratic equation using the discriminant?

AI Teacher (09:31 AM)

Certainly! For a standard quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0 (where a ≠ 0), the discriminant is given by D = b² − 4ac:

  • If D > 0: Two distinct real roots exist.
  • If D = 0: Two equal real roots exist.
  • If D < 0: No real roots exist (roots are complex/imaginary).

Card 1: What is Ohm's Law?

Answer: At constant temperature, the current (I) flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) across its ends: V = I × R.


Card 2: Define Photosynthesis.

Answer: The process by which green plants use sunlight, carbon dioxide (CO2), and water (H2O) to synthesize glucose and release oxygen.

Answer: In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides: a² + b² = c².
